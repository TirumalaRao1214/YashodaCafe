About Cafe Yashoda

Located conveniently near KEA Hospital in Vavilalapally, Karimnagar, Cafe Yashoda has grown into the town's beloved café hub. We pride ourselves on offering a comprehensive menu designed for every craving and every generation.

From hot herbal teas, strong coffees, healthy ragi and millet bowls, to loaded burgers, thick milkshakes, and indulgent sundaes — quality and hygiene are at the heart of our kitchen.
