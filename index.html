About Cafe Yashoda

Nestled in the heart of Karimnagar, Telangana, Cafe Yashoda has been serving the community with warmth, freshness and authentic flavours. From the first sip of our signature Yashoda Tea to the last spoonful of our Ice Cream Delights — every bite tells a story of quality and care.

We believe good food creates brighter days. Come in, sit down, and let us take care of the rest.
